# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Wantere to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the people using the product.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, code, issues and pull requests that do
not follow this code of conduct, and may ban contributors who repeatedly break
it.

Report unacceptable behaviour by opening a confidential report through
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) contact channels. Reports are reviewed and handled
privately.

This document is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
